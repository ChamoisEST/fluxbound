# Welcome to Fluxbound

Fluxbound is a technology mod focused on energy,
teleportation, machinery, and connected infrastructure.

## Getting Started

Your first teleportation setup will use an Arrival
Anchor and a Basic Handheld.

As you progress through Minecraft, you'll unlock
Advanced and Quantum teleportation equipment.
