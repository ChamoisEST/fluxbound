# Arrival Anchor

The Arrival Anchor is Fluxbound's first stationary teleportation machine.

## Purpose

An Arrival Anchor serves as a destination for teleportation devices.

It cannot initiate teleportation by itself.

## Energy

The Arrival Anchor stores FE supplied by compatible external energy sources.

Each standalone Arrival Anchor provides:

- 100,000 FE capacity
- 200 FE/t maximum input
- No passive FE consumption

These values can be configured by the server.

### Energy connections

Energy can enter through the bottom and four sides.

The top face does not accept FE connections because it is reserved for arriving players.

Multiple connections share one total input limit.

### Charging

Handheld charging is planned but not implemented yet.

Arrival Anchors will eventually charge handhelds using their stored FE.


## Multiblocks

Adjacent Arrival Anchors will eventually form rectangular multiblock structures.

Larger structures will provide increased energy storage, input speed, and handheld charging speed.

Multiblock formation is not yet implemented.
